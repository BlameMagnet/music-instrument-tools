# Music & Instrument Tools

Reference site for music practice utilities, music-theory aids, and multi-brand instrument serial-number year lookup.

Site: [https://heiguozhi.wang/en/](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/)

## Tools

- [Tuner](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/tuner.html) — Real-time microphone pitch detection for guitar, bass, violin, and other instruments. Supports adjustable A4 reference pitch.
- [Metronome](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/metronome.html) — Online metronome with time signatures, tap tempo, and optional voice cues for practice and rhythm training.
- [Circle of Fifths](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/circle-of-fifths.html) — Interactive circle of fifths covering church modes, key relationships, relative majors/minors, and common harmonic motion. Intended for composition, arrangement, and theory study.
- [Tone Playground](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/tone-playground.html) — Online signal-chain sandbox for electric guitar and bass: pedals, amp heads, cabinets, and DI, with wet/dry comparison. Selected heads support NAM sample tones; microphone or uploaded dry audio may also be used. Instructional simulation only—not an official product of any gear brand.
- [Chord Dictionary](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/chord-dictionary.html) — Guitar and ukulele chord reference with major, minor, seventh, suspended, and other types. Provides open and movable shapes, SVG diagrams, tone lists, and audio playback.
- [Fretboard Visualizer](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/fretboard.html) — Interactive fretboard for guitar, bass, and ukulele. Supports multiple tunings, scale and chord templates, custom fret ranges, and audio preview.
- [Pitch & Frequency](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/pitch-frequency.html) — Twelve-tone equal-temperament frequency table with adjustable A4 (392–470 Hz). Lists theoretical frequencies for all semitones from C0 to C10, with per-note playback.
- [Scale Explorer](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/scale-explorer.html) — Catalogue of more than twenty scale types, including major, minor, pentatonic, blues, symmetric, and other collections. Includes fretboard visualization and audio.
- [Chord Progression Builder](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/progression-builder.html) — Constructs chord progressions from scale degrees. Supports custom sequences, common presets, BPM control, and audio playback.
- [Drum Machine](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/rhythm-patterns.html) — Style-based drum pattern library (rock, funk, samba, reggae, and others) with BPM control and visual playback.
- [Interval Ear Training](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/ear-training.html) — Interval recognition trainer with multiple interval sets and difficulty levels. Supports ascending, descending, and harmonic presentation, with accuracy statistics.
- [Wire Gauge Converter](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/wire-gauge-converter.html) — Conversion among AWG, SWG, metric/imperial diameter, and cross-sectional area for instrument and audio cabling.
