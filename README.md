# Music & Instrument Tools

A free website for musicians: practice utilities, music-theory helpers, and serial-number year lookup for guitars and related gear.

Open the site: [https://heiguozhi.wang/en/](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/)

Also available in [简体中文](./README.zh-Hans.md) and [繁體中文](./README.zh-Hant.md). The website itself offers the same pages in Simplified Chinese, English, and Traditional Chinese.

## Tools

- [Tuner](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/tuner.html) — Tune with your microphone; works for many instruments.
- [Metronome](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/metronome.html) — Practice with a clear click and optional voice cues.
- [Circle of Fifths](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/circle-of-fifths.html) — See how keys relate and common chord moves.
- [Tone Playground](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/tone-playground.html) — Stack pedals, amps, and cabs and try guitar/bass tones online.
- [Chord Dictionary](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/chord-dictionary.html) — Guitar and ukulele shapes, notes, and audio.
- [Fretboard Visualizer](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/fretboard.html) — Explore notes, scales, and chords on guitar, bass, or ukulele.
- [Pitch & Frequency](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/pitch-frequency.html) — Note frequencies with adjustable A4, plus playback.
- [Scale Explorer](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/scale-explorer.html) — Browse 20+ scales on the fretboard with audio.
- [Chord Progression Builder](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/progression-builder.html) — Build and play progressions from scale degrees.
- [Drum Machine](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/rhythm-patterns.html) — Style-based patterns with BPM control.
- [Interval Ear Training](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/ear-training.html) — Practice recognizing intervals by ear.
- [Wire Gauge Converter](https://heiguozhi.wang/en/tools/wire-gauge-converter.html) — Convert AWG, SWG, diameter, and cross-section for audio wiring.

Lookups are for estimating year and related details—not a certificate of authenticity. Tone Playground is a teaching-style simulator, not an official product from any gear brand.
